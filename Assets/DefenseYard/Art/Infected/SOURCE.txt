Selected source meshes: Zombie_Chubby (walker), Zombie_Basic (runner), Zombie_Arm (juggernaut).
Supplied animations retained: Idle, Walk, Run, Run_Arms, Punch, Death, HitReact.
The game uses Idle / Walk / Punch; the runner plays Walk at a quicker rate. Movement remains controlled by gameplay.

Downloaded glTF sources from the public CC0 mirror:
https://github.com/agentkaerf/FreeModels/tree/main/Zombie%20Apocalypse%20Kit%20-%20March%202024
Converted to FBX with Blender 4.5.3; removed the unused Icosphere helper and unused animation clips.
Added Unity materials, controllers, sizes, grounded prefab offsets and gameplay colliders.
License.txt is the mirror's original bundled license; its pack title is a template.
