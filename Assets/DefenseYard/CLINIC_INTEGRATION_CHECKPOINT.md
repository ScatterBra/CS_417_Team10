## Implemented contract

Medicine provenance is created only by a paid, supported Prep Bench recipe. All recipe inputs are checked and debited before notifications; a one-use ticket snapshots outputs and the source patient/bench/recipe/generation. Generic Add, initial stock and debug Medicine create unproven units. FIFO units preserve provenance through the player's inventory, actual cabinet stock/take and treatment refill. Generic spending consumes corresponding units. External station/cabinet stat edits conservatively invalidate stored proof.

Treatment checks the actual patient, occupied pad, positive healing and stock. A successful dose reports its exact unit and before/after patient HP after committed resource effects. Reentry cannot double-spend or double-stock; inventory notifications isolate listener exceptions. Reset during a craft, transfer or dose invalidates old proof without undoing already committed gameplay effects. Disable cancels treatment. Clinic health remains separate from Defense base health.

DEF_ClinicReadiness requires the configured patient's fresh 50-to-100 chain using the configured Medicine recipe and eligible scoped benches, delivered through eligible scoped treatment stations. A direct/debug health or Medicine change cannot itself complete readiness; a discontinuity in the verified health chain fails closed. New full runs require fresh/reloaded Clinic state; Defense retry does not call BeginSession and retains earned prerequisites. Temporary adapter disable observes the chain but postpones publication until re-enabled. A full session reset invalidates in-flight and stored provenance.

Production staging does not pre-place free training buildings. The adapter uses existing MED_Building.OnBuilt, exact building definitions and the configured buildingsContainer to bind newly placed bench/station instances. Foreign areas, previews and old buildings cannot supply the fresh binding. No Clinic placement/progression/UI rewrite was made.
